Overview

A story-driven 2D precision platformer for PC about a lost squid navigating an unfamiliar world, guided by a kind ferret lady determined to get him home, and reminding the broken what it means to love.

Gameplay

Have you ever wanted a game where you do things, and more things happen? Introducing PORTALHOPPER, a 2D precision platformer about a little squid trying to find his way home after being ripped away to an unfamiliar world.

But that's not all! The game also has crazy, silly, wacky, goofy, haha, funny, lol, rofl characters you can conversate with! There's also this cool dash mechanic that I added on a whim which I think is pretty cool, he turns blue when you dash. You can also do things like... walk... and... jump. Ok, I think I've covered all the gameplay stuff.

Story

Splat, a small squid with little memory of his past, finds himself far from home in a world that functions entirely differently from his own, known as The Bridge, and sets out to return home.

Along the way he meets Ramona; a kind Ferret lady who becomes determined to aid Splat in any way possible, all while a dangerous force works to stop Splat from leaving by any means necessary.
